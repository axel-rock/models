# Code of conduct

We want a focused and welcoming technical community.

Be respectful. Discuss the work, not the person. Assume questions are made in
good faith, but accept correction when an action causes harm. Harassment,
threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, and disclosure of private
information are not acceptable.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict
participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns
privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and
confidentially as practical.
